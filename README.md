## About

I am a professor and researcher at UNEMAT working at the intersection of **scientific computing, software engineering, and applied artificial intelligence**. My work focuses on turning mathematical models, scientific questions, and real-world problems into traceable, testable, and reproducible computational systems.

My academic background includes an **M.Sc. in Science from Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica (ITA)** and a **Licentiate degree in Computing from UNEMAT**. My current activities combine teaching, academic leadership, research, extension, and applied software development.

A simple workflow summarizes much of what I aim to build:

> **scientific research → model → implementation → validation → reproducible software**

## Research & technical interests

- **Computational electromagnetics** — waveguides, cavities, eigenvalue problems, and electromagnetic propagation.
- **Numerical methods** — FEM, Whitney/Nédélec edge-element formulations, EFG/EFGMI, and engineering simulation.
- **Scientific & high-performance computing** — C/C++, Python, parallel computing, benchmarking, and numerical reproducibility.
- **Artificial intelligence & computer vision** — image acquisition and analysis, applied AI, automation, and research support systems.
- **Embedded systems & IoT** — ESP32, Raspberry Pi, sensors, and experimental prototypes.
- **Applied software** — digital health, biodiversity, education, automation, and research-oriented systems.
